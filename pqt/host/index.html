    Your cards

    Don’t Say It — one per line: WORD: banned, banned, banned

    Top Answers — the question on one line, then each answer with its points (Answer, 30). Leave an empty line between questions. The samples are made up: replace them with your own survey results.

    Hi, My Name Is… — the answer on the first line, then one clue per line. Several clues? They show one at a time and earn fewer points as more are shown. Empty line between people.

    Spelling Bee — one per line: WORD | meaning | example sentence

    Written Round — the question, then the answer on the next line. Empty line between questions.

    Rapid Fire — the question, then the answer. Add image: /path.png on a third line to show a picture. Empty line between questions.

    Discussion — one topic per line.
