Music

A multidisciplinary creative with over a decade of experience in fields he simply says, has to do with humanity, lifestyle, and creativity.

Born and raised in Abuja, Bemshima founded BLACC Universal to bring every medium he works in under one roof, with one point of view. He has worked and continues to work extensively in design, marketing, media, and technology, and in advancing the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
