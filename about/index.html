Founder

Bemshima

Creative Lead & Founder

A multidisciplinary creative with over a decade of experience across broadcast media, fashion, music production, video production and graphic design.

Born and raised in Abuja, Bemshima founded BLACC Universal to bring every medium he works in under one roof, with one point of view. Alongside the house, he works in Nigeria’s film exhibition and distribution industry, and builds products, games and experiences through GXMECITY.
