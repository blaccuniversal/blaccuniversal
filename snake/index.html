How to play
  1. Steer. Swipe on a phone, or use the arrow keys or W A S D. Turn on the on-screen pad from the pause menu if you prefer buttons.
  2. Eat the gold squares. Each one makes you longer and scores points equal to your speed.
  3. Grab the bonus. Every fifth meal a spinning gold diamond appears. The faster you reach it, the more it’s worth.
  4. Don’t crash. Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game. In mazes with gaps, you can go off one edge and come back on the other.
  5. Beat your best for each maze and speed.
