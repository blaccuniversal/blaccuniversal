The story

From ₦5,000 and a craving.

Kabira Ilo, Chef Kabs, started with stir-fry pasta orders on WhatsApp while at the University of Ibadan. Today her kitchen runs from a restaurant in Bodija, alongside Kabs Bar, Kabs Diner and Sunday at Kabs.

  1. Start₦5,000 of stir-fry pasta orders on WhatsApp.
  2. 2021A kiosk on the University of Ibadan campus.
  3. 2024Certified as a chef.
  4. Dec 2024A five-bedroom duplex in Bodija becomes the restaurant, with a pool.
