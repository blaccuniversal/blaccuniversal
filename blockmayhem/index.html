How to play
  1. Move the paddle. Drag anywhere on a phone. Mouse or arrow keys on a computer.
  2. Launch. Tap, click or press Space.
  3. Multiply. A ball through a gold ×3 orb becomes three. A +3 orb adds three balls. Collect stars.
  4. Grab the gold rush. Now and then a ×20 orb appears and blinks out after a few seconds, or a countdown orb drops from ×10 to zero. Hit it in time.
  5. Clear every block. Grey steel never breaks. Gold blocks take more than one hit.
  6. Spend stars on boosts. ×3 every ball, +3 new balls, or a wide paddle. Keys 1, 2, 3.
  7. Three lives. You lose one when every ball falls. Finish with more lives for a better rating.
