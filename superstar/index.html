How to play

Rap a line every bar

  1. Pick a beat. Choose one of the built-in beats, set the tempo, or tap your own.
  2. Follow the ball. It bounces on every beat. Four beats make one bar, and each bar is one line.
  3. Land the rhyme. The word sits on beat 4. Say whatever you like on beats 1 to 3, then hit the word as the ball lands on it.

Levels

  • FreestyleNo words. Just you, the beat and the bars.
  • BeginnerEvery rhyme shown, in pairs (AABB).
  • IntermediateOnly the first word of each pair. You find the rhyme.
  • AdvancedOnly the second word. Set it up so your first line rhymes into it.
  • ExpertFour bars on one sound (AAAA). Only the last word is shown.

Tips

  • Use headphones when recording, so your mic only picks up your voice.
  • Start slow. Drop the tempo until landing every word feels easy, then speed up.
  • Play the daily set to keep your streak going.
