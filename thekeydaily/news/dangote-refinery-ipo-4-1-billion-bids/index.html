Dangote Refinery IPO Draws About $4.1 Billion in Bids

Demand for shares in Africa's biggest refinery came in at roughly three times the target, setting up a landmark listing in Lagos.

By The Key Daily Desk11 November 20261 min read

The Dangote refinery's initial public offering has drawn about $4.1 billion (around N5.85 trillion) in share applications, roughly three times what it set out to raise. Bloomberg called it Africa's top IPO.

The subscription period was extended, and the listing on the Nigerian Exchange is now expected in December.

Analysts at Nairametrics say the listing, along with a steadier naira and strong foreign reserves, could push the total value of Nigerian stocks past N230 trillion by the end of the year.
