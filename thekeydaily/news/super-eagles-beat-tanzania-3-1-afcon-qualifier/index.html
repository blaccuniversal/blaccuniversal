Arokodare Double Fires Super Eagles Past Tanzania in Uyo

Nigeria won 3-1 in the first of two AFCON 2027 qualifiers against Tanzania this week.

By The Key Daily Desk13 November 20261 min read

Nigeria beat Tanzania 3-1 at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo on Friday, 13 November, in an AFCON 2027 qualifier.

Alhassan Yusuf put the Super Eagles ahead in the third minute. Tanzania drew level through Msanga in the 36th minute, but Tolu Arokodare scored twice after the break, in the 52nd and 60th minutes, to settle it.

Coach Eric Chelle's squad meets Tanzania again in the return leg on Tuesday, 17 November.
