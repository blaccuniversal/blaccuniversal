COP31 Opens in Antalya With a Focus on Delivering Climate Promises

Nearly 200 countries are meeting in Türkiye until 20 November. Climate finance for developing countries is high on the agenda.

By The Key Daily Desk9 November 20261 min read

The UN climate conference COP31 opened in Antalya, Türkiye, on 9 November and runs until 20 November. Nearly 200 countries are taking part, and more than 90,000 people are expected.

Türkiye's environment minister, Murat Kurum, is COP31 president. Australia's Chris Bowen oversees the negotiations under a co-presidency agreed at COP30.

Rather than chasing a single landmark deal, this summit focuses on turning existing pledges into action. Key issues include faster access to climate finance for developing countries, clean energy, adaptation and preparing for a strong El Niño.
