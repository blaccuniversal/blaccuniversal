Godzilla Minus Zero Sets a North American Record With $31.6 Million Debut

The Japanese sequel opened at No. 1, the biggest start ever for a foreign-language live-action film other than The Passion of the Christ.

By The Key Daily Desk8 November 20261 min read

Godzilla Minus Zero topped the North American box office on the weekend of 6 to 8 November with an estimated $31.6 million, according to studio figures reported by the Associated Press.

That beat the previous foreign-language live-action record held by Hero ($17.9 million). Among live-action foreign-language films, only The Passion of the Christ opened bigger, with $83.8 million in 2004.

The film cost under $20 million to make and has strong reviews, with 90% on Rotten Tomatoes and an A CinemaScore. Warner Bros.' The Cat in the Hat came second with $20.5 million.

Overall ticket sales rose nearly 97% from the slow Halloween weekend before it.
