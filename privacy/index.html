Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Last updated 11 November 2026

This page explains what blaccuniversal.com collects and what we do with it. The short version: we only keep what you choose to send us, we never sell it, and the site itself sets no cookies.

What you send us

  • Build with us and Say hello. Your name, email and message, plus anything optional you add: subject, phone, city and topics.
  • Notify me (Projects and Shop). Your email address.
  • Shop orders. Your name, email, phone, delivery address, country, size, quantity and any notes.

We use this only to reply to you, to deliver your order, and to tell you about the things you asked to hear about. Forms reach our inbox through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that handles them under its own privacy policy.

Payments

No card or bank details are entered on this website. After you order, we email you the bank transfer details.

Cookies and your device

This website sets no cookies. It saves three small preferences on your own device, and they never leave it: the currency you picked in the Shop, the latest exchange rates (so prices load quickly), and whether you chose light or dark on Bemshima’s page. Clearing your browser’s data for this site removes them.

Counting visits and plays

We use GoatCounter to count page visits and song plays on Bemshima’s page. It uses no cookies and doesn’t identify you. We only see totals, such as how many times a song was played and in which countries.

Other services on the site

Some pages include players from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode), Audiomack and Spotify. When you play something in them, those services may set their own cookies under their own policies. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and Shop exchange rates come from open.er-api.com. Neither receives any personal details from you.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your information. We only share it when it’s needed to deliver your order (for example, with the courier who brings it to you) or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and orders for as long as we need them to reply, deliver and keep proper business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or stop hearing from us. Use the Say hello form and we’ll take care of it. Under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
